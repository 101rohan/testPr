Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Purpose-built sections with sensible defaults. Replace the placeholder copy with your own.

Blazing fast

No frameworks, no build step. A few kilobytes of HTML, CSS, and JS that load instantly.

Responsive by default

Mobile-first layout that adapts cleanly from phones to wide desktop screens.

Accessible

Semantic markup, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support out of the box.

Easy to customize

One stylesheet with CSS variables — change the accent color or spacing in seconds.

FAQ & pricing included

Conversion-ready sections — pricing tiers, FAQ accordion, and a final call to action.

Yours to keep

Plain files you own completely. Edit in any editor, host anywhere, no lock-in.

How it works

Live in three steps

From download to deployed in minutes, not days.

1. Make it yours

Edit the copy, swap the accent color in styles.css, and drop in your logo.

2. Add your content

Fill in real testimonials, pricing, and FAQs. Every section is clearly marked.

3. Publish anywhere

Upload the three files to any static host — no build, no server, no fuss.
